How we work

Four rules we stick to

These four rules apply to every enquiry, regardless of treatment and clinic.

Rule 01

No offer without findings

We quote no price before partner doctors have reviewed your records. An offer without findings cannot deliver on site what it promises, and that is exactly what we want to avoid.

Rule 02

Everything in writing

You receive services, costs, dates and cancellation terms in writing before you commit. Anything not in the cost plan has not been agreed.

Rule 03

One contact person

You have one dedicated contact who knows your story, in German, Turkish, English or Arabic. You don't have to explain yourself again at every step.

Rule 04

Advising against is part of the job

If a treatment does not make sense, is too risky or is better done at home, we tell you. Even if that means we arrange nothing.
