FAQ

Questions about getting in touch

How quickly will I get a reply?

Your contact person usually gets back to you within one working day. The initial medical assessment is generally available 24 to 48 hours after we receive complete records.

Can I send my findings straight away?

Yes. You can send findings, X-rays and photos by email or WhatsApp after your enquiry. We treat them confidentially and share them only with the reviewing partner doctors.

Is the advice really free of charge?

Yes. The initial assessment, advice and cost plan cost you nothing and commit you to nothing. Costs only arise once you agree to the cost plan and book an appointment.

Which languages can I use?

English, German, Turkish and Arabic. At the clinic, too, we make sure you understand the consultation and instructions in your language.
