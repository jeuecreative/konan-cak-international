Treatments

Konan Cak International arranges treatment in six specialties at vetted partner clinics in Türkiye [and other countries]. For each area, this page shows which procedures are possible, how long you stay and which documents we need in advance.

Principle

Findings first, then the offer.

Which treatment suits you is not decided by a catalogue, but by a doctor looking at your records. That is why we quote no flat-rate prices before partner doctors have seen your findings, photos or X-rays.

This way you avoid offers that suddenly become more expensive on site because the findings turn out differently. And you learn early on if a treatment does not make sense in your case.

Area 01

Hair transplant

A hair transplant moves your own hair follicles, usually from the back of the head, to thinning areas. It is an option for hereditary hair loss if there is enough donor hair and the hair loss has stabilised.

Partner doctors assess whether this applies to you using photos from several angles. How dense the result will be depends on donor hair, hair structure and healing; we discuss this openly beforehand.

  • FUE

    Individual follicles are extracted and placed into prepared channels.

  • DHI

    Follicles are implanted directly with an implanter pen.

  • PRP

    Platelet-rich plasma treatment, offered alongside the transplant.

Area 02

Dentistry & implants

Dental implants replace missing teeth with artificial roots onto which crowns or bridges are later fitted. We also arrange crowns, bridges and veneers for extensive restoration needs.

Implants usually require two stays, because the bone needs to heal after placement. How many implants make sense and whether bone grafting is needed only becomes clear from the X-ray.

  • Implants

    Artificial tooth roots made of titanium or ceramic.

  • Crowns and bridges

    Tooth replacement on natural teeth or implants.

  • Veneers

    Thin ceramic shells on the front of the teeth.

Area 03

Aesthetic plastic surgery

We arrange rhinoplasty, eyelid surgery and body contouring with specialists in plastic surgery. Each of these procedures is an operation with risks, which are discussed in detail before you decide.

Partner doctors clarify in advance whether your wish is realistic and the procedure medically justifiable. We only arrange aesthetic procedures for patients aged 18 and over.

  • Rhinoplasty

    Changing the shape or function of the nose.

  • Eyelid surgery

    Removing excess skin on the upper or lower eyelid.

  • Body contouring

    For example a tummy or arm lift after major weight loss.

Area 04

Ophthalmology & laser eye surgery

Laser eye procedures correct short-sightedness, long-sightedness and astigmatism. If laser treatment is not possible, lens procedures can be an alternative.

Whether your eyes are suitable is decided by a preliminary examination that measures corneal thickness, among other things. It takes place at the clinic before the procedure; existing findings help with the initial assessment.

  • LASIK

    Correction beneath a previously created corneal flap.

  • SMILE

    Minimally invasive procedure through a small incision.

  • Lens procedures

    Implantation of an artificial lens, for example for high prescriptions.

Area 05

Orthopaedics & joint replacement

We arrange hip and knee replacements and other orthopaedic procedures. The basis is current X-ray or MRI images and a description of your symptoms.

With joint replacement, the time after surgery is decisive. We therefore plan the first rehabilitation phase on site and the transition to physiotherapy at home.

  • Hip replacement

    Artificial hip joint for advanced osteoarthritis.

  • Knee replacement

    Partial or total knee prosthesis.

  • Other procedures

    For example on the spine or shoulder, depending on findings.

Area 06

Bariatric surgery

Gastric sleeve and gastric bypass reduce the size of the stomach and can help with severe obesity when other measures have not been enough. They are permanent procedures that require a lifelong change in diet and ongoing aftercare.

Partner doctors review BMI, pre-existing conditions and previous therapies in advance. We only arrange treatment if long-term aftercare at home has also been arranged.

  • Gastric sleeve

    A large part of the stomach is permanently removed.

  • Gastric bypass

    Food is routed past part of the stomach and small intestine.

  • Nutritional support

    A plan for the weeks after surgery.

Limits

When we are not the right partner

We do not arrange emergency treatment or procedures that partner doctors consider unjustifiable after review. Anyone looking for an appointment without a review of findings, or a price „on the spot“, is in the wrong place with us.

We do not arrange aesthetic procedures for minors. For serious pre-existing conditions, we often recommend treatment close to home and tell you so openly.

Tell us what you need.

Send us your records. Partner doctors review them free of charge and without obligation, usually within 48 hours.
