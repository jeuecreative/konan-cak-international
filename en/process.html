How treatment with us works

With Konan Cak International, treatment abroad follows six stages: enquiry, medical review, cost plan, travel, treatment and aftercare. For each stage you can see here how long it takes, who is involved and what you need to do yourself.

Stage 01 · Day 1

Enquiry

You describe your concern via the form, by phone or on WhatsApp. If you have findings, X-rays or photos, send them straight away; this speeds up the review.

Your personal contact person gets back to you within one working day. They answer open questions and tell you which documents are still missing.

Result of this stageYour records are complete and go to the partner doctors.

Key facts
DurationDay 1
InvolvedYou and your contact person
Your effortapprox. 10 minutes for the form and documents

Stage 02 · 24 – 48 hours

Medical review

Partner doctors in the relevant specialty review your records. They assess whether treatment is an option, which procedure fits and whether further examinations are needed beforehand.

If treatment is not an option or is too risky, you find out at this stage – with reasons.

Result of this stageAn initial medical assessment, free of charge and without obligation.

Key facts
Duration24–48 hours
InvolvedPartner doctors, your contact person
Your effortNone; answer follow-up questions if needed

Stage 03 · Days 2 – 4

Cost plan

Based on the assessment, you receive a written cost plan. It names the treatment, the clinic, the services included and everything that is not included.

You have time to review the plan, ask questions or get a second opinion. Only when you agree do we reserve an appointment.

Result of this stageA binding, written cost plan.

Key facts
DurationDays 2–4
InvolvedClinic, your contact person
Your effortRead the cost plan and decide

Stage 04 · Once you choose a date

Travel

You choose a date and we coordinate it with the clinic. On request, we arrange your hotel and transfers from the airport to the clinic; you usually book the flight yourself.

Before departure you receive a travel plan with addresses, appointments and the phone number of your contact person on site.

Result of this stageAppointment, accommodation and transfers are confirmed.

Key facts
Durationonce you choose a date
InvolvedClinic, your contact person
Your effortBook your flight, work through the checklist

Stage 05 · 1 – 7 days

Treatment

On arrival you are picked up and accompanied to the clinic. Before the procedure, the treating doctor holds an informed-consent consultation, with interpreting if needed.

Your contact person is available throughout your stay. Before you leave, you receive your records and instructions for the time afterwards.

Result of this stageTreatment completed; discharge documents and aftercare plan in hand.

Key facts
Duration1–7 days, depending on the procedure
InvolvedClinic team, your contact person on site
Your effortConsultation, treatment, recovery

Stage 06 · Up to 12 months

Aftercare

After you return home, we stay in touch. We remind you of check-ups, pass photos or questions on to the clinic and help coordinate with doctors at home.

If you have symptoms after treatment, your contact person is your first point of contact. In an emergency, please call 112.

Result of this stageYour recovery is documented and supported.

Key facts
Durationup to 12 months
InvolvedClinic, your contact person, your doctors at home if needed
Your effortAttend check-ups, give feedback

Costs & payment

When you pay what

You only pay once you have agreed to the written cost plan. The initial assessment and our advice are free of charge.

Payment 01

Deposit to the clinic

A deposit of [X %] is due to reserve your appointment. You pay it directly to the clinic; the cancellation terms are set out in the cost plan.

Payment 02

Balance on site

You pay the balance at the clinic after the informed-consent consultation. You receive an invoice that you can submit to your health insurer if applicable.

Payment 03

Hotel, flight, transfers

The cost plan states explicitly which of these are included. You usually book and pay for flights yourself.

Preparation

Checklist before departure

These points should be taken care of before you leave:

  • Travel documents

    Valid passport or ID card; entry requirements checked.

  • Medical records

    Findings, X-rays and cost plan, printed and digital.

  • Medication and pre-existing conditions

    A list of your medication; only stop blood thinners after consulting your doctor.

  • Insurance cover

    Check your travel health insurance; planned treatments are often excluded.

  • Companion

    Recommended for major procedures, especially for the journey home and the first few days.
