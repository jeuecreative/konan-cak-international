Frequently asked questions about treatment abroad

Here we answer the questions patients ask us most often. Can't find yours? Write to us.

What does Konan Cak International do?

Konan Cak International arranges and accompanies medical treatment at vetted partner clinics in Türkiye [and other countries]. We obtain an initial medical assessment, prepare a written cost plan, organise your travel and accompany your aftercare for up to 12 months.

Is Konan Cak International a clinic?

No. Treatment is carried out by our partner clinics. We are your contact between you and the clinic and represent your interests.

Which treatments do you arrange?

Hair transplants, dentistry and implants, aesthetic plastic surgery, laser eye surgery and lens procedures, orthopaedics and joint replacement, and bariatric surgery. We also arrange preventive check-ups.

How much does treatment cost?

That depends on your findings, so we only quote prices after the medical review. You then receive a written cost plan listing all included and excluded services.

How much does your service cost?

The initial assessment and advice are free of charge for you.

How do you select partner clinics?

We only work with clinics we have vetted in advance, including for [accreditations, doctors' qualifications, experience with international patients, hygiene standards].

Is treatment abroad safe?

Every medical treatment carries risks, at home and abroad. We reduce them through a medical pre-assessment, vetted clinics, consultations in your language and accompanied aftercare. No one can guarantee a treatment outcome.

Will my health insurance cover the costs?

Planned treatment in Türkiye is usually not covered by statutory health insurance, and aesthetic procedures generally never are. Please clarify this in writing with your insurer before treatment.

How long do I need to stay?

Depending on the treatment, between 2 days (for example laser eye surgery) and about 2 weeks (for example joint replacement). Dental implants usually require two stays a few months apart.

What happens if problems arise after I return home?

Your contact person is available for up to 12 months after treatment and coordinates next steps with the clinic and your doctors at home. In an emergency, please call 112 immediately.

In which language will I be looked after?

In English, German, Turkish or Arabic, from your first enquiry to aftercare. The consultation at the clinic also takes place with interpreting if needed.

Which documents do I need for the initial assessment?

That depends on the area: photos for hair transplants and aesthetic procedures, a panoramic X-ray for dental treatment, X-ray or MRI images for orthopaedics. You can also enquire without documents; we'll tell you what is missing.
